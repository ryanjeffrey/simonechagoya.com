A special one-of-a-kind necklace, handmade in December 2025. A gender-neutral design, perfectly understated, at a modern length of 23 inches.

African Jade, also called Dendritic Jade, comes from South Africa. The stones are often green, with dark, tree-like (dendritic) patterns. The dendrites are mineral formations of iron and manganese oxides, creating unique natural art. Stones are translucent or opaque green, orange, or sometimes white or grey. African Jade is not a true jade (jadeite) but a Grossular Garnet, prized for its beauty and believed to have properties of healing and prosperity.
