Jasper, in all forms, is considered a powerful healing stone. Fancy jasper is known for its incredible color palette. Jasper is an opaque, solid or patterned variety of cryptocrystalline quartz which consists of very tiny quartz crystals colored by various mineral impurities.

Jasper is found all over the world, with certain colors or patterns unique to particular locations. Jewelry use of jasper goes back into the early history of civilization. The ancient Egyptian priests carved it into jewelry, and the Minoans used it for seals. The term "jasper" comes from the Greek word iaspi, meaning "spotted stone."

Fancy jasper’s rich colors blend and complement each other creating a sense of tranquility. Jasper represents encouragement and inner strength, calming and grounding energy, a sense of well-being, and focuses the wearer on the present.
