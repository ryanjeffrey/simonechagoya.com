The Verde Necklace is for the woman who finds power in renewal and grows boldly toward abundance.

The color green is associated with good luck and prosperity, making green opal a stone for bringing good fortune. Verde is the Spanish word for green.

Natural Green-Brown Opal is a variety of common opal known for its rich earth tones. This natural gemstone is composed of silicon dioxide, the same mineral found in quartz crystal. Sourced primarily from Nevada, USA, its color ranges from pale ivory to deep earthy greens and browns, shaped by trace elements of iron and nickel. Unlike precious opals, green opal carries a quiet, grounded beauty, rich in color and texture rather than flash.
