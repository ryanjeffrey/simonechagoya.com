African Turquoise is found across the continent of Africa, with significant deposits in Namibia, South Africa, Egypt, Madagascar, and Zimbabwe, prized for its earthy greens and blues with dark matrix patterns. A form of jasper, which is a microcrystalline quartz, not true turquoise, its distinctive coloring comes from copper and iron compounds, creating patterns that mimic actual turquoise.
